Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before confidential data changes hands.

A written scope controls every paid engagement.

What size business is a fit?

M. Kathleen is initially optimized for small and growing organizations and bounded payroll projects. Larger companies may fit when the assignment, access, and authority are clearly defined.

Which payroll schedules are supported?

Weekly, biweekly, semimonthly, monthly, quarterly, and off-cycle work can be considered.

Can M. Kathleen work in our current platform?

Potentially. Fit depends on licensing, access, client permissions, scope, and security controls.

Will M. Kathleen take over all employer responsibility?

No. The employer remains responsible for accurate source data, classification, funding, approvals, registrations, deposits, notices, and legal compliance.

Can you fix a payroll emergency?

Resolution depends on access, authority, platform availability, banking windows, and third parties. Emergency availability and outcomes are never guaranteed.

How is confidential information handled?

Do not send Social Security numbers, bank details, employee files, passwords, medical information, tax returns, or payroll exports through first-contact email. A secure method must be agreed after fit and scope are confirmed.

Is M. Kathleen a law firm, CPA firm, or tax agency?

No. Payroll operations support is not legal advice, tax advice, a financial-statement audit, benefits brokerage, worker-classification counsel, or agency representation.
